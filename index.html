Frequently Asked Questions

What courses do you offer?
We offer coaching for 5th to 10th standard (English & Semi-English Medium), 11th & 12th Science (PCB/PCMB), Commerce, Vedic Mathematics, and Spoken English courses.
What are the class timings?
Regular batches run from Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Special Sunday moderator sessions are also conducted for board exam preparation.
How do I enroll my child?
You can visit our campus directly, call us at +91 9011395913, or send an inquiry through the website. We'll guide you through the admission process.
What is the fee structure?
Fee structure varies by course and standard. Please contact our office or download the fee structure from the Downloads section.
Do you provide study material?
Yes, we provide comprehensive study material, practice papers, and previous year question papers to all enrolled students.
What is the batch size?
We maintain small batch sizes (15-20 students) to ensure personalized attention and effective learning.
Do you offer demo classes?
Yes, we offer free demo classes for new students. Contact us to schedule a demo session.
